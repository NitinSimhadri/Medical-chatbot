🔒 Privacy & Security

Your health data is stored locally in your browser for privacy. In a production app, this would be encrypted and stored securely on HIPAA-compliant servers. Consider exporting important data regularly for backup.

How to use

  1. Upload or add medical documents (prescriptions, reports) to your Health Vault for safekeeping.
  2. Use encryption (set FERNET_KEY) if you want files encrypted at rest on the server.
  3. You can export files to share with clinicians; keep backups of your encryption key if enabled.
