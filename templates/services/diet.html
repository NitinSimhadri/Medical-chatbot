How to use

  1. Enter your age, height, weight and activity level and click "Get Plan" to calculate estimated calories.
  2. Use the dietary filters to tailor suggestions (Veg / Non Veg / Gluten-free) and enable protein focus if desired.
  3. Click "Order Protein Meals" or "Find Nearby Gyms / Yoga" to discover local options, or use homemade suggestions if none are found.
