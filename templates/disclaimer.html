Please read carefully before using our services

MediCare Pro is an AI-powered healthcare assistant designed to provide general health information and support. However, it is crucial to understand the limitations and appropriate use of this service.

🚨 Critical Warning

This application is NOT a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult qualified healthcare providers for medical concerns.

What This Service Provides:

  • General health information and educational content
  • Symptom tracking and basic health monitoring tools
  • Appointment booking assistance with healthcare providers
  • Health data organization and record keeping
  • General wellness and lifestyle guidance

What This Service Does NOT Provide:

  • Medical diagnosis or treatment recommendations
  • Emergency medical care or urgent interventions
  • Prescription of medications or treatments
  • Interpretation of medical test results
  • Legal or insurance advice

Emergency Situations: If you are experiencing a medical emergency, severe symptoms, or believe you may be having a heart attack, stroke, or other life-threatening condition, please call emergency services (911 in the US, 112 in Europe, or your local emergency number) immediately. Do not wait for or rely on this application.

Medical Decisions: Any decisions about your health should be made in consultation with qualified medical professionals who can provide personalized advice based on your complete medical history, examination, and appropriate testing.

Data Privacy: While we take privacy seriously, this is a demonstration application. In a production environment, ensure you understand how your health data is stored, used, and protected.

Accuracy: While we strive for accuracy, medical information can change, and errors can occur. The information provided should not be considered comprehensive or error-free.
