About This Medical Chatbot

What it is: This Medical Chatbot is a demonstration conversational assistant that combines a retrieval-augmented approach (vector search over medical documents) with a language model to provide informative responses to health-related queries.

Why use it: It helps surface relevant information from a curated document collection quickly, summarizes findings, and offers general educational guidance. It is useful for learning, research, and rapid information lookup.

How to use: Start a conversation on the chat page. Ask symptom-related questions, request explanations of conditions, or use the service pages (BP & Heart Rate, BMI Calculator, Diet Planner, Skin & Hair Care) for quick tools and guidance. When using any measurement tools, provide accurate inputs (height, weight, device readings) for best results.

Services offered (free):

  • Chat-based information and document-backed answers
  • Blood pressure & heart rate guidance (informational; use certified devices for clinical readings)
  • BMI calculator
  • Simple diet planner with caloric estimate
  • Skin & hair care image preview (informational only)
  • Additional educational resources and lifestyle tips

Important: This tool is for informational and educational purposes only. It does not provide a medical diagnosis or replace advice from a licensed healthcare professional. For medical emergencies or clinical decisions, consult a qualified provider.
